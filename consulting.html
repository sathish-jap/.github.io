How it works

A straightforward process

No lengthy procurement. No generic frameworks delivered off the shelf.

1

Discovery Call

A focused conversation to understand your team's size, current AI maturity, industry context, and what you're actually trying to achieve. This shapes everything that follows.

2

Workflow & Readiness Review

A structured review of your key workflows to identify where AI adoption makes real sense — based on volume, complexity, and your team's capability to sustain it.

3

Upskilling Strategy & Roadmap

A practical adoption plan: who needs to be trained, what tools to adopt, in what sequence, and how to measure progress. Delivered as a working document your team can act on.

4

Training Delivery (Optional)

If the strategy calls for it, custom training sessions are delivered for your team — live, online, with hands-on components tailored to your actual workflows and tools.
