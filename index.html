01 / Programs

Three training streams. One goal — teams that can apply AI in real work.

Each stream is modular and built for a specific audience and outcome. Topics below are representative — every engagement is tailored to your team, your industry, and what you're actually trying to drive.

Stream 01

Foundational AI Literacy

Technical & Non-Technical

Build a clear mental model of what modern AI is, what it can do today, and where it quietly fails. The starting point for almost every team.

  • Capability mapping — where AI excels, fails, and shouldn't be used
  • Prompting that moves real business outcomes
  • Privacy, data risk, and responsible use in enterprise contexts
  • Building your personal AI toolkit at work
Stream 02

AI Adoption Strategy

Senior Leaders & Managers

A leader's view of AI adoption — how to spot real opportunities, avoid expensive experiments, and lead teams through the change with clarity. Built for decision-makers, not implementers.

  • Building an AI-ready organization, function by function
  • Opportunity mapping — where AI actually pays off
  • Governance, policy, and responsible-use frameworks
  • Measuring AI ROI without fooling yourself
Stream 03

AI Agents & Agentic Systems

Technical & Non-Technical

Beyond chatbots — what agents actually are, how they fit into real business processes, and how to design them with the oversight your organization will demand. The next frontier, made concrete.

  • Understanding AI agents and their capabilities
  • Designing agents around real business processes
  • Components of agentic systems: models, tools, and memory
  • Guardrails, permissions, and safe agent behavior
