Sathishkumar Sadhasivam

GenAI Consultant & Trainer · Founder, Kogniva.ai

Sathish has spent ~20 years designing and delivering technology solutions across some of the world's most complex enterprise environments — including Emirates Airline, IBM, and Accenture.

That background gave him an unusual vantage point: he's seen how large organisations actually adopt technology (slowly, inconsistently, and often without the right enablement) — and where AI fits differently.

Today, he focuses entirely on helping IT professionals and business leaders bridge the gap between knowing about AI and actually using it — through hands-on training that skips the hype and gets straight to what works.

Why Kogniva?

Most AI training produces awareness. It tells people what's possible. What it doesn't do is sit with them while they actually build — debug a broken prompt, rethink an architecture, figure out what Claude does well and where it falls short.

Kogniva exists because adoption requires more than a lecture. It requires guided practice, real feedback, and someone who's been inside enterprise environments long enough to know which use cases will actually survive contact with reality.
