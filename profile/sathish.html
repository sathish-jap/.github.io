01 / Training

Four learning streams. One goal - teams that can confidently apply AI in real work.

Each stream is modular. Topics below are representative — every engagement is tailored to your industry, team profile, and the outcomes you're trying to drive.

STREAM 01

Foundational AI Literacy

Technical & Non-Technical

Build a clear mental model of what modern AI is, what it can do today, and where it quietly fails — so teams can use it with judgment instead of hype.

  • Capability mapping — where AI excels, fails, and shouldn't be used
  • Prompting that moves real business outcomes
  • Privacy, data risk, and responsible use in enterprise contexts
  • Building your personal AI toolkit at work
STREAM 02

AI Adoption Strategy

Senior Leaders & Managers

A leader's view of AI adoption — how to spot real opportunities, avoid expensive experiments, and lead teams through the change with clarity.

  • Building an AI-ready organization, function by function
  • Opportunity mapping — where AI actually pays off
  • Governance, policy, and responsible-use frameworks
  • Measuring AI ROI without fooling yourself
STREAM 03

AI Agents & Agentic Systems

Technical & Non-Technical

Beyond chatbots — what agents actually are, how they fit into real business processes, and how to design them with the oversight your organization will demand.

  • Understanding AI agents and their capabilities
  • Designing agents around real business processes
  • Components of agentic systems: Models, Tools, and Memory
  • Guardrails, Permissions, and Safe Agent Behavior
STREAM 04

AI-Assisted Software Development

Technical & Non-Technical

For developers, a new way of working. For non-developers, a path to building working software for the first time. Both meet at the same place — shipping real things.

  • Building working software without writing code
  • The AI-native developer workflow with Claude Code
  • Spec-driven development — thinking before building
  • Code quality and review in an AI-assisted world
